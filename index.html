Frequently Asked Questions

It is an international destination dance festival held in Boracay, Philippines, bringing together dancers from around the world for six days of workshops, socials, performances, parties, competitions and island experiences.
SBKZ stands for Salsa, Bachata, Kizomba and Zouk - the four dance styles at the heart of the festival.
The main festival days are May 7-9, 2027, as part of a full six-day run from May 5-10: a Pre-Party on May 5, an Artists & DJs Night on May 6, the main festival May 7-9, and a Farewell Party on May 10. The venue is Boracay Ocean Club Resort & Spa, Station 3, Boracay Island, Philippines.
The Full Festival Pass is best for the complete experience (workshops, socials, shows and parties). The Performer Pass is for registered performers, and the Party Pass covers designated social parties. Details are subject to the official ticket terms.
The program is designed to welcome a range of levels. Specific level assignments will be announced with the final schedule.
Yes - the Party Pass provides access to designated social parties and selected evening events. It excludes workshops unless purchased separately.
Accommodation is not included by default. Travel and accommodation packages will be announced separately.
Fly to Manila or Cebu, connect to Caticlan or Kalibo airport, then take a boat transfer to Boracay and a local transfer to Station 3.
The boat party is separate fee.
Register for a Performer Pass and submit your details. Performance participation is subject to approval and technical requirements.
Registration will open through the official channels. Fees are PHP 1,000 (Amateur) and PHP 1,500 (Open Level), subject to available slots.
Absolutely this is an international festival. Please verify your travel, visa and entry requirements before your trip.
Comfortable dance attire and shoes, plus tropical resort wear for pool, beach and boat events. Specific themed-party details will be shared closer to the event.
All updates will be published on the official website and social channels.
